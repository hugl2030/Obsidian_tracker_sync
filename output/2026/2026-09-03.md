Abstract 
                   The grain boundary instability induced by defects and tensible strain impedes the realization of high-performance inverted perovskite solar cells (PSCs). Here, we propose a flexible bidirectional multisite additive strategy to stabilize perovskite. (6-(2,5-Dioxo-2,5-dihydro-1H-pyrror-1-yl) hexanehydrazide hydrochloride (PHHCl) with a hydrazide cation, three carbonyl groups, and a flexible alkyl chain is leveraged to manipulate the GBs of perovskite. | **日期** | 2026-09-03 |

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<details>
<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   Composite solid polymer electrolytes (CSPEs) have emerged as promising candidates for next-generation lithium metal batteries. However, the spatial evolution of the solid electrolyte interphase (SEI) at solid-state interfaces remains poorly understood despite its critical role in lithium metal stability. Herein, the anion-trapping layered double hydroxide (LDH) is introduced to poly(ethylene oxide)-based CSPEs to regulate Li+ transport and stabilize the lithium/electrolyte interface. The regulated Li+ flux promotes homogeneous lithium deposition and the formation of a robust, multifunctional SEI, thereby suppressing lithium dendrite growth and stabilizing the interface. This study provides direct visualization of SEI evolution in CSPEs and clarifies its role in dendrite-free all-solid-state lithium metal batteries.

<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   Catalytic amyloids are proposed evolutionary precursors of modern enzymes, yet the active-site dynamics underlying their distinct catalytic properties remain unclear. Here, we combine azide infrared probes and two-dimensional infrared (2D-IR) spectroscopy to characterize the active-site dynamics of primitive catalytic amyloids and compare them with human carbonic anhydrase II (CAII), a representative modern enzyme. The Zn2+-bound azide in amyloids exhibits a ∼30 cm–1 blue shift and a 2-fold longer vibrational lifetime than free azide, establishing vibrational lifetime as a sensitive reporter of metal coordination. Center Line Slope (CLS) analysis reveals the absence of picosecond dynamics in bound azide, indicating that rigid amyloid scaffolds lack the rapid hydration and side-chain motions present in CAII. This contrast reveals two evolutionarily divergent catalytic strategies: conformational selection versus tuned dynamics.
