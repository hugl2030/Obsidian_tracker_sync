### 2. Scalable Synthesis of Interfacial Regulators for Durable Zinc Powder Batteries

> **用于耐用锌粉电池的界面调节器的可扩展合成**

| | |
|---|---|
| **期刊** | ACS Energy Letters |
| **作者** | Bin Liu, Tao Liao, Tianchen Li, Krish N. Jayarapu et al. |
| **日期** | 2026-09-11 |
| **DOI** | [10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01779](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01779) |

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<details>
<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   Zn powder is a promising anode for aqueous Zn-ion batteries because of its tunability and compatibility with industrial manufacturing, but parasitic reactions and uncontrolled deposition limit practical use. Here, we develop a tandem plasma-enhanced powder synthesis technology (TaPPS) that integrates chemical vapor deposition with powder spheroidization to yield zincophilic, defective graphene-coated copper powders (Gr@Cu) at a throughput of 3.36 kg h−1. When incorporated into Zn powder electrodes, Gr@Cu particles redistribute charge and provide preferential Zn nucleation sites, enabling thermodynamic and kinetic regulation of Zn deposition. This powder-level interfacial regulation enables highly reversible plating/stripping over 4000 cycles with 99.91% Coulombic efficiency at 5 mA cm−2, while full cells operate stably for over 1500 cycles. Beyond Zn powder electrodes, Gr@Cu also functions as an artificial interfacial layer on Zn metal anodes, highlighting TaPPS as a scalable powder-engineering platform for developing functional interfacial regulators for durable Zn electrochemistry.

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

<details>
<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   Hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) is a pivotal atmospheric oxidant whose reactivity in aqueous microenvironments, such as aerosols, is highly sensitive to humidity. This sensitivity is postulated to arise from water’s ability to distort the H–O–O–H dihedral angle (ω), a key conformational coordinate governing H2O2’s electronic excitation energy (S1) and O–O bond cleavage propensity. However, a direct molecular-level mechanism linking discrete hydration-shell architecture to ω and, consequently, to electronic tuning has remained elusive. Here, we employ high-resolution rotational spectroscopy to characterize the microsolvation of H2O2 by probing the H2O2–(H2O)3–5 clusters. The topology of the water hydrogen-bonding network, not merely the hydration number, strongly modulates ω. The results show that the formation of 3D cyclic and prismatic networks in H2O2–(H2O)4 reduces this angle to approximately 90°, while the small clusters (n ≤ 3) preserve ω close to the isolated-molecule value (∼115°). In contrast, this angle increases to about 130° in the pentahydrate. Consequently, the S1 energy increases for the tetrahydrates and decreases for the pentahydrate relative to that of isolated H2O2. This work reveals how the hydrogen-bonding topology within the hydration shell selectively manipulates molecular conformation and reactivity.

| **日期** | 2026-09-11 |
| **DOI** | [10.1021/jacs.6c15204](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c15204) |

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<details>
<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   Particulate nitrate (NO3–) and organic nitrates (pON) represent important inorganic and organic reservoirs of reactive nitrogen, respectively, yet direct chemical conversion from NO3– to pON remains unresolved. Herein, using Born–Oppenheimer molecular dynamics (BOMD) simulations, we reveal the molecular mechanism underlying acid-gated inorganic-to-organic nitrate (IN-to-ON) conversion at the air–water and air–ice interfaces. Adsorption of aldehydes at the air–water interface, combined with acid-driven protonation, markedly enhances carbonyl electrophilicity, enabling nucleophilic addition of NO3– and yielding pON on a picosecond time scale. This interfacial mechanism exhibits broad chemical generality: it operates across typical anthropogenic acids (nitric and sulfuric acids, HNO3 and H2SO4), the marine-derived iodic acid (HIO3), and diverse carbonyl substrates ranging from C1–C3 aldehydes to the dicarbonyl glyoxal. Strikingly, analogous reactivity at the air–ice interface demonstrates that this chemistry extends beyond liquid aerosol water to ice particles in polar regions and the upper atmosphere. These findings establish a molecular-level bridge between aerosol NO3– and pON, identifying acid-mediated interfacial IN-to-ON processing as a potentially overlooked in situ formation pathway for pON. This interfacial chemistry may help explain the persistent underprediction of atmospheric pON burden and advance our understanding of global nitrogen chemical cycling.
