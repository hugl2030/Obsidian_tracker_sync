### 1. Making 1000 Wh/kg LiNiO2 Cathode Nearly Strain-Free by Complex-Concentrated Doping

> **通过络合物浓缩掺杂使1000 Wh/kg LiNiO2阴极几乎无应变**

| | |
|---|---|
| **期刊** | ACS Energy Letters |
| **作者** | Yaqi Jing, Zhen Wang, Rui Zhang, Lei Wang et al. |
| **日期** | 2026-10-25 |
| **DOI** | [10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01681](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01681) |

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<details>
<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   High-energy batteries require cathodes with greater capacity and specific energy. Ni-rich-layered oxides approach the performance limit of LiNiO2 (LNO), yet its practical use is hindered by severe chemomechanical degradation. Near 4.2 V, the H2-H3/H4 transition drives substantial c-axis contraction, volume shrinkage, O1 formation, oxygen loss, and rapid capacity decay. Here, we show that only 5% cobalt-free complex concentrated doping stabilizes LNO without sacrificing energy density. The modified LNO reduces c-axis strain from 7.4 to 2.1%, exhibits near-zero-strain behavior, and delivers 258 mAh•g–1 and 973 Wh•kg–1. In pouch cells, it retains 80% capacity after 1800 cycles and shows markedly improved thermal stability. These results demonstrate that complex-concentrated doping at low dopant loading can suppress strain and phase transformations at the full-Ni limit, providing a broadly applicable strategy for stabilizing high-energy intercalation electrodes while preserving their intrinsic capacity and specific energy during prolonged electrochemical cycling.

Abstract 
                   Ultra-wide-bandgap (UWBG) metal halide perovskite solar cells are essential for multi-junction solar cells with higher power conversion efficiency (PCE) potentials. In this work, we report the dual functions of 5-iodoisophthalic acid (I-BDC) to suppress phase segregation and uncoordinated Pb2+ related defects to enhance UWBG perovskite cell performance and stability. ### 3. rGO-MXene 2D Interfacial Engineering for Enhanced Efficiency and Stability of Perovskite Solar Cells and Modules

| **日期** | 2026-10-25 |

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   In this work, we report an innovative interface engineering strategy combining bidirectional chemical anchoring and electronic bridging via a 2D reduced graphene oxide (rGO) and MXene conductive network. This hierarchical 2D structure suppresses the aggregation and stacking of single-layer MXene nanosheets while mutually compensating for individual charge transport limitations. Specifically, the abundant functional groups passivate interfacial defects (Pb2+ and I–) and bond with the PCBM layer via coordination, hydrogen bonding and π–π interactions. This synergistic architecture lowers the electron energy offset. Consequently, PSCs incorporating the rGO-MXene interlayer achieve a champion power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 25.96% (certified 25.05%) and retain 96.43% of initial efficiency after 1000 h in ambient air. Furthermore, 10 × 10 cm2 large-area PSM yields a high PCE of 20.25%, maintaining 96.05% of initial efficiency under maximum power point tracking for 963 h.

### 2. Bridging Spectroscopy
and Reactivity: Identifying
the True Reactive CO Species in Electrocatalytic CO Oxidation

> **桥接光谱学

电催化CO氧化中真正的反应性CO物种**

| | |

| **期刊** | JACS |
| **作者** | Tian-Yi Yang, Ya-Jun Huang, Hao-Fei Geng, Hai-Sheng Su et al. |
| **日期** | 2026-10-25 |
| **DOI** | [10.1021/jacs.6c13006](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c13006) |

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<details>
<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   In situ electrochemical vibrational spectroscopy has revolutionized our understanding of electrocatalysis. However, establishing a rigorous correlation between spectroscopic signatures and reaction kinetics is still very challenging because high-coverage surface species may dominate the signal, whereas the truly reactive participants may be short-lived or spectroscopically silent. To disentangle spectators from true reaction participants, we developed a high-sensitivity spectroelectrochemical platform that integrates the rotating disk electrode with surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (RDE-SERS). This approach enables the simultaneous acquisition of reaction kinetics and interfacial molecular information under well-defined mass transport conditions. Using this versatile approach, we investigated the benchmark carbon monoxide oxidation reaction (COOR) on the Pt surface, which enabled decoupling between the spectroscopically observable species and the catalytically active species. Despite persistent and intense Raman signatures of adsorbed CO (COad) in the diffusion-limited region, COad predominantly acts as a spectator that blocks surface sites without effectively participating in the reaction, as confirmed by the isotope substitution experiments. Conversely, the oxidation current is sustained by the continuous flux of solution-phase CO (COsol), which is identified as the kinetically relevant reactant. These findings offer a new perspective on the roles of adsorbed species versus solution-phase species during dynamic electrochemical reactions. Collectively, the RDE-SERS methodology is established as a reliable strategy for identifying true reaction pathways, bridging molecular-level spectroscopic identification with macroscopic reactivity in complex electrocatalytic systems.

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<details>
<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   Delayed emission, a long-lived nonexponential photoluminescence tail, is widely observed in quantum dots (QDs), but its microscopic origin and relationship with blinking and the quantum-confined Stark effect (QCSE) remain unclear. Here, we combine single-QD spectroscopy, time-gated photon analysis, and Monte Carlo simulations across CsPbX3 perovskite, CdSe-based, and InP-based QDs to identify a potential mechanism. Delayed emission correlates with band-edge carrier-trapping blinking, but is weak in QDs dominated by trion-mediated Auger blinking, suggesting delayed emission is not universally tied to charge-separated states. Delayed photons originate from nanosecond-scale carrier trapping and detrapping at nonradiative recombination centers: transiently trapped carriers return to the band edge and recombine radiatively. Time-gated analysis distinguishes this process from QCSE-driven intensity-lifetime fluctuations on slower blinking time scales. Furthermore, we demonstrate that delayed photons compromise the single-photon purity at a fixed excitation repetition rate. These findings establish a general framework of delayed emission in QDs.

Abstract 
                   Heatwaves are becoming more frequent, intense and prolonged as the climate warms, placing stress on the electricity infrastructure underpinning modern societies. Yet how rising temperatures erode grid capacity remains under-quantified. Existing power-flow models either oversimplify thermal effects or become computationally intractable when resolving them in detail. Here we introduce a unified, computationally efficient framework that jointly captures temperature-dependent demand, generator derating and spatially resolved transmission-line thermal limits within an alternating-current optimal power-flow formulation. Applying it to the Western European system with open-access grid, high-resolution weather reanalysis and demand data, we simulate hundreds of projected heatwave scenarios for 2026–2030. Across these represented grid configurations, the analysis reveals substantial national disparities: the Spanish grid exhibits temperature-induced bottlenecks and load shedding, whereas the German grid remains comparatively resilient. Heatwave-aware power-flow analysis thus uncovers structural bottlenecks and should be integrated into grid planning and adaptation as heatwaves intensify under climate change.

Injection of pure spin current into superconductors remains a major challenge in superconducting spintronics. Previous studies have primarily focused on spin-polarized quasiparticles and spin-triplet supercurrents, while vortices—ubiquitous topological defects in type-II superconductors—have been theoretically proposed as alternative carriers of spin angular momentum, yet direct experimental evidence is still lacking. Here, we report the vortex-mediated spin current injection in NbSe
                     2 
                    /LiAl
                     2 
                    Fe
                     3 
                    O
                     8 
                    (LAFO) bilayer, an Ising superconductor/ferrimagnetic insulator heterostructure. Under an out-of-plane temperature gradient and an in-plane magnetic field, the NbSe
                     2 
                    /LAFO bilayer shows a pronounced thermoelectric peak near the upper critical magnetic field, which has the opposite sign to the conventional vortex Nernst signal observed in a single-layer NbSe
                     2 
                    . The sign reversal suggests that the vortex flow induced by spin current injection is opposite to the flow driven by the temperature gradient, which is consistent with theoretical mechanisms including spin-vorticity transmutation and the inverse vortex spin Hall effect. By mapping the field–temperature phase diagram, we reveal that the spin current injection occurs exclusively in the vortex liquid phase of NbSe
                     2 
                    . Absence of the thermoelectric signal above the superconducting transition temperature further rules out the quasiparticle contribution. Our results establish vortices as efficient carriers of spin information in superconductors, opening a new route towards vortex-mediated superconducting spintronic devices.
