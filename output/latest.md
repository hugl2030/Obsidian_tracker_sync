### 1. Decoupled Water Electrolysis with an Air-Stable Zwitterionic Azo Mediator

> **使用空气稳定型两性离子偶氮介质进行去耦合水电解**

| | |

| **日期** | 2026-10-27 |
| **DOI** | [10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02938](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02938) |

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<details>
<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   Decoupled water electrolysis (DWE) separates H2 and O2 evolution, improving safety and flexibility under intermittent renewable electricity, but requires durable, oxygen-tolerant redox mediators. Here we evaluate 4,4′-azo-bis(1-pyridinium-3-propane-sulfonate) (ABPS), a zwitterionic azo molecule, as an electron-coupled proton buffer for acidic DWE. Its azo/hydrazo core supports reversible two-electron/two-proton chemistry at 0.81 V vs RHE, while zwitterionic substituents provide high aqueous solubility and suppress crossover through low-cost sulfonated poly(ether ether ketone) membranes. Reduced H2ABPS shows no detectable oxidation after 90 days in air or during &gt;5 h of continuous pure-O2 bubbling. Symmetric flow-cell cycling over 1000 h yields a capacity-fade rate below 1% year–1. With commercial catalysts at room temperature, decoupled flow cells reach 1.2 A cm–2 and operate for &gt;200 h at 500 mA cm–2 with near-quantitative Faradaic efficiencies while producing high-purity H2 and O2. These results establish zwitterionic azo chemistry as an oxygen-tolerant, durable platform for high-rate decoupled hydrogen production.

### 2. Microphase-Engineered Homologous Ion-Exchange Layers for High-Current-Density Bipolar Membrane Electrolysis

> **用于高电流密度双极膜电解的微相工程同源离子交换层**

| | |
|---|---|
| **期刊** | ACS Energy Letters |
| **作者** | Jingjing Tu, Zongliang Wan, Xiaojiang Li, Fen Luo et al. |
| **日期** | 2026-10-27 |
| **DOI** | [10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02765](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02765) |

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<details>
<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   Bipolar membranes (BPMs) maintain separated acidic and alkaline environments via water dissociation at the junction, but high-current-density operation remains challenging because rapid hydrated H+/OH– migration away from the junction must be coupled with sufficient water replenishment toward it. A transport imbalance between these opposing processes causes interfacial dehydration and limits long-term operation. Here, we develop microphase-engineered homologous ion-exchange layers to balance this coupled transport. These layers are fabricated from homologous multiblock poly(biphenyl alkylene) ionomers sharing a common backbone and block architecture, promoting interlayer compatibility and interconnected ionic domains. The hydrated pathways accelerate water replenishment and ion transport, thereby maintaining a stably hydrated junction. The resulting BPM enables pure-water electrolysis at 2.11 V at 1000 mA cm–2 and sustains high-purity hydrogen production for over 1100 h at 500 mA cm–2. This work establishes molecularly engineered ion-exchange layers as an effective approach for improving mass transport in high-current-density BPM systems.

Abstract 
                   Nitrogen-centered radicals possessing substantial anionic character have remained highly challenging to isolate because of their intrinsic electronic instability in the absence of metal coordination. Herein, we report a family of stable N-heterocyclic carbene (NHC)-derived α-diimine radical cations that constitute a rare class of persistent organic radicals featuring pronounced anionic nitrogen-centered radical character. **🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<details>
<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   Sodium-ion batteries are attractive candidates for low-cost and scalable energy storage in which hard carbon (HC) anodes are used. However, sodium (Na) metal plating in HC anodes at voltages above the plating potential remains a critical problem. The location and spatial distribution of crystalline sodium (c-Na) metal nanoparticles, the clusters associated with Na storage in HCs, are important for understanding this underpotential plating behavior but remain unclear. Here, we implement a cryogenic electron microscopy (cryo-EM) and electron tomography (cryo-ET) workflow that directly localizes c-Na metal clusters in sodiated HC samples: cryo-ET maps nanoscale three-dimensional (3D) distributions, while cryo-EM resolves local structure and chemistry. | | |

| **日期** | 2026-10-27 |
| **DOI** | [10.1021/acs.nanolett.6c03773](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.nanolett.6c03773) |

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<details>
<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   Ratchets provide directed transport without global biased forces via parity-breaking combinations of nonequilibrium driving with dissipation or nonlinear dynamics. They play a central role down to the quantum level in molecular motors, colloidal particles, microbiological processes, semiconductor devices, optical lattices, and photonic structures, where nonequilibrium is usually achieved by dynamic potentials or couplings. Here, we address the question of whether transport can be achieved by only using time-dependent dissipation while keeping all potentials and couplings static. Notably, the rectified transport increases with larger local dissipation. Floquet theory uncovers low-loss directional transport at suitable driving frequencies, which is linked to linear quasienergy bands. These regions are separated from nonresonant behavior by sharp transitions with characteristic exceptional points in the spectrum. A plasmonic Floquet-dissipative ratchet is studied experimentally by using real- and Fourier-space leakage radiation microscopy.
