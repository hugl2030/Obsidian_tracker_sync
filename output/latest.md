### 2. Pressure-Driven Topological Reconfiguration toward Fast-Charging Hard Carbon with Plateau-Capacity Retention

| **日期** | 2026-10-16 |

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   Conventional carbonization of semi-coke induces excessive ordering of aromatic skeletons, producing dense graphitic structures that hinder Na+ intercalation and suppress low-potential plateau capacity. Here, we develop a rapid thermomechanical coupling sintering strategy using moderate pressure to drive π–π interactions and topological reconfiguration of aromatic units. This process suppresses excessive graphitization while constructing interconnected, twisted pseudo-graphitic domains with abundant closed nanopores. At 1000 mA g–1, PTR-HC reaches 80% SOC in ≈14.4 min and retains 90.1% of its plateau capacity, enabled by abundant Na+ storage sites and rapid diffusion pathways. Kinetic analyses reveal accelerated Na+ transport and a thin, NaF-rich solid-electrolyte interphase with fewer organic components. In situ XRD and Raman attribute slope capacity to surface/defect adsorption and intercalation and the plateau capacity to quasi-metallic Na clusters in closed pores. This work establishes pressure-driven topological engineering as a promising strategy for upgrading semi-coke into fast-charging hard carbon with high plateau-capacity retention.

### 3. Grain Boundary Character Governs Lithium Deposition Uniformity on Polycrystalline Copper

> **晶界特征控制多晶铜上的锂沉积均匀性**

| | |
|---|---|
| **期刊** | ACS Energy Letters |
| **作者** | Minhyung Kwon, Seungyun Jeon, Dong Yeon Kim, Uk Jung Kang et al. |
| **日期** | 2026-10-16 |
| **DOI** | [10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02640](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02640) |

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<details>
<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   In anode-free Li metal batteries, a bare current collector must be converted into a reversible Li metal anode during the first charge, making Li nucleation decisive for reversibility. However, how current collector microstructure governs Li nucleation and growth remains poorly understood, since prior studies focused on single-crystal surfaces to identify favorable crystallographic orientations, leaving grain boundaries unexplored. Here, we identify grain boundary density and character in copper current collectors as key regulators of Li deposition uniformity. Systematic annealing decouples grain size from surface chemistry, revealing that deposition uniformity improves with copper grain coarsening but saturates as disordered boundaries are eliminated during grain growth, leaving predominantly low-energy configurations. Atomistic simulations show disordered boundaries impose high barriers to Li adatom migration, whereas ordered boundaries and adjacent low-index terraces provide low-barrier pathways for redistribution. Copper foils engineered beyond this threshold sustain uniform Li plating across diverse electrolytes and improve cyclability in anode-free cells.

Abstract

Here, we investigate third-harmonic generation in flat and crumpled silicon nanomembranes of 15 and 35 nm using polarization-resolved third-harmonic generation microscopy with ab initio calculations.
