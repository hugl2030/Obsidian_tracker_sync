### 1. Spiro-OMeTAD-Inspired Molecular Design: Electron Tunneling Meets Halogen Bonding for Robust Buried Interfaces

> **受Spiro-OMeTAD启发的分子设计：电子隧穿与卤键结合，实现稳健的埋置界面**

| | |
|---|---|

| **日期** | 2026-11-14 |
| **DOI** | [10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02986](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02986) |

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   Tin-based perovskite solar cells (TPSCs) are limited by fragile buried interfaces and uncontrolled nucleation. These get exacerbated when conventional hole-transport materials such as spiro-OMeTAD are used with dopants, especially in inverted p–i–n devices, leading to accelerated ion migration and poor stability. To overcome these limitations, we introduce BDTP-4D, a spiro-OMeTAD-inspired bis-dithienopyrrole molecule designed to mimic its merits while avoiding dopant-induced instability. Applied as a self-assembled monolayer (SAM) atop NiOx, BDTP-4D was co-sensitized with triphenylamine derivatives possessing targeted functionalities (–Br, –SMe, –OMe), which modulated hole-transport-layer surface energy and directed perovskite nucleation and crystallization. This yielded pinhole-free films with large, homogeneous grains atop the co-sensitized electrodes. Mechanistic analysis revealed complementary buried-interfacial processes: NiOx–Br electronic coupling and halogen-bonding between Br and Sn perovskite (BDTP:Br), and sulfur-mediated passivation (BDTP:SMe). This mixed-SAM strategy reproduces the benefits of doped spiro-OMeTAD while eliminating interfacial instability, thereby defining a generalizable molecular design principle for robust buried interfaces across emerging photovoltaics.

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Abstract 
                   Electrocatalytic nitrate reduction to ammonia is often constrained by the proton-coupled hydrogenation of adsorbed nitrite (*NO2), a step commonly accelerated through hydrogen spillover and local reactive-hydrogen (*H) supply. This hydrogen-centered view, however, treats *NO2 largely as a passive intermediate and overlooks whether its migration can reconstruct the interfacial hydrogen-bond-network environment required for downstream hydrogenation. Here, we show that directional *NO2 spillover couples site-specific intermediate transport with interfacial hydrogen-bond-network reorganization. In a Cu–Ag dual-site model interface, nitrate activation and *NO2 formation occur preferentially on the Cu sites, whereas migrated *NO2 species are stabilized and hydrogenated on the Ag-associated sites. In situ spectroscopy, kinetic isotope measurements, distribution-of-relaxation-time analysis, and theoretical simulations reveal that this Cu-to-Ag relay enriches K+-associated interfacial water, reinforces hydrogen-bond connectivity, and facilitates *H delivery for *NO2 hydrogenation. The optimized CuAg N-doped carbon (CuAg–N-C) achieves near-unity Faradaic efficiency (FE) for NH3 with a yield rate of 411.8 mmol g–1 h–1 and retains 83% FE at 1 A cm–2 in a flow cell. The catalyst further retains substantial nitrate-to-ammonia recovery in simulated and authentic industrial water matrices. More broadly, this work establishes *NO2 spillover as a dynamic interfacial mechanism linking intermediate transport, water-network organization, and proton-coupled nitrogen conversion, providing a framework for sustaining molecular-scale selectivity under chemically complex reaction environments.

Abstract 
                   Chiral scattering in nanostructures emerges from interference between electric and magnetic modes, yet its associated modal strength, arrangements and spectral concentration cannot be inferred from structural handedness alone. Using plasmonic nanorod dimers as a model system, we combine full-field electromagnetic simulations and single-particle circular differential scattering (CDS) spectroscopy to reveal how rotational and translational reconfigurations reshape modal overlap, coupling, and spectral organization even within an established handed architecture. ### 1. Antiferroelectric hafnia down to the 2D limit

> **反铁电氧化铪降至二维极限**

| | |
|---|---|
| **期刊** | Science |
| **作者** | Xin Li, Guodong Ren, Haidong Lu, Kartik Samanta et al. |
| **日期** | 2026-11-12 |
| **DOI** | [10.1126/science.ady5526](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.ady5526) |

**🔑 核心价值**：（暂无）

**关键词**：—

<details>
<summary>📄 英文摘要</summary>

Antiferroelectricity is a material property characterized by alternating electric dipoles spontaneously ordered in antiparallel directions. Antiferroelectrics are promising for energy storage, solid-state cooling, and memory technologies; however, these materials are scarce, and their scalability remains largely unexplored. In this work, we demonstrated that single-crystalline hafnia, a lead-free complementary metal-oxide semiconductor (CMOS)–compatible material, exhibits antiferroelectricity under compressive-strain conditions. We observed antiparallel sublattice polarization and stable double hysteresis in single-crystalline (111)-oriented epitaxial lanthanum (La)–doped hafnia films grown on yttrium-stabilized zirconia and showed that the antipolar orthorhombic phase of hafnia adheres to the Kittel model of antiferroelectricity. Compressive strain strengthens the orthorhombic order in thinner La-doped hafnia films, achieving a very high ordering temperature of 850°C in the two-dimensional limit, highlighting hafnia’s potential for advanced antiferroelectric devices.

</details>
